## How To Begin

Let's begin with Klaviyo.

Login into our Klaviyo account and go into one of our email flows. We will edit the email subject lines using Subject Line Assistant, and Klaviyo AI technology, one of their latest new releases.

In this example, we are editing the subject line for the Welcome flow to provide a better direction for the specific content. Providing more information and details to an AI service can help to improve its performance and accuracy.

Here are the next steps you need to take to successfully and most effectively get the results you want:

1. Select flow type. This will ensure that the generated subject lines are relevant.
2. Enter the brand name.
3. Provide a short description of the brand or the email content. Make sure to keep it short, and personal and provide any important information the customers may need.
4. Click 'Generate' and watch the magic happen.
5. You will see three generated subject lines, and the option to generate more if you are not satisfied with the ones available.

As you can see in my example, I have just generated several ideas for my Welcome Flow content email subject line. If you're already using Klaviyo, using their AI tool to help generate subject and preview lines will come naturally. Test this process when creating new flows, and test them against your own subject lines to see which ones perform better.

## Comparing Klaviyo AI to ChatGPT

Now, let's compare Klaviyo's subject lines with what ChatGPT will create for us.

In order to do this, we need a free account on ChatGPT. After logging in, you will see a search box at the bottom of the screen, where you can enter a descriptive phrase or a question.

Now sit back, and let the magic happen…

I can keep pressing "Regenerate Response" to get more results and look for those which satisfy me the most. You can also add as many or as few details as you want, and give it parameters to use too. For example, if you wanted to mention a specific promotion or a word you can include that in the description.

Now it's time to test them!
