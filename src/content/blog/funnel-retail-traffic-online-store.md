## How Can QR Codes Be Used For Marketing?

QR (Quick Response) codes serve as a bridge connecting physical retail locations to digital channels. These scannable codes enable businesses to move in-store customers onto email databases by directing them to online platforms.

The strategy involves advertising the advantages of shopping online compared to in-store experiences, coupled with incentive offers. Once customers join mailing lists through this method, brands can create conversion opportunities through targeted email sequences.

## Displaying Your QR Code

QR codes should be positioned in high-traffic areas within retail spaces, with designs reflecting brand aesthetics. Display options range from elegant in-store iPad installations to simpler flyer placements near checkout areas.

Effective flyers include engaging copy, basic QR code instructions for unfamiliar users, and visible incentive offers that encourage email sharing.

## Landing Page Strategy

Scanning QR codes directs customers to landing pages where they enter contact information. These pages typically offer dual pathways:

- Email entry leading to Klaviyo integration
- Phone number capture through platforms like Attentive

Follow-up messages confirm subscriptions and provide discount instructions, emphasizing that offers apply exclusively to online purchases.

## Developing Email Flows in Klaviyo

Brands should establish multi-email sequences triggered by signup events, including:

- Initial welcome message
- Follow-up for unopened emails
- Sequential daily emails

Using separate lists prevents duplicate messaging for existing subscribers while ensuring new retail converts receive promotional content.

## Converting Retail Shoppers to Online Buyers

Messaging should emphasize online-exclusive advantages:

- Expanded clearance selections
- Buy-now-pay-later options (Klarna, AfterPay)
- Exclusive offers and larger product variety
- VIP sales and new release access

Social proof in the form of reviews demonstrates brand credibility and builds customer confidence in online shopping experiences.

## Conclusion

QR codes provide a seamless way to bridge the gap between your physical retail presence and your online store. By implementing this strategy with well-designed landing pages and targeted email flows, you can effectively convert in-store traffic into loyal online customers.
