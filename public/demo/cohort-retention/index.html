What This Means

Of 2,120 customers acquired in November 2025, 150 made a second purchase within 90 days (7.1%). This is 29% below the 10% benchmark and below your 10.8% average across all cohorts. This cohort ranks #6 of 8.

Contributing Factors to Investigate

  • Holiday-season cohort — customers acquired in November 2025 are disproportionately gift buyers whose next natural purchase occasion may be 6–12 months out.
  • Below-average AOV ($84 vs $101 median) suggests discount-driven or entry-level buyers. Price-sensitive customers are harder to retain without ongoing incentives — consider whether the margin supports it.
  • Repurchase rate is down vs November 2024 (9.0% → 7.1%). Same season, so this is a real change: check acquisition channels, product mix, or promotional strategy.
  • 90-day LTV is down more than 15% vs November 2024: new customers from this season are worth less than they were a year ago.
  • First-order value dropped 17% vs November 2024 — possible shift toward lower-margin products or heavier discounting.
  • For context: 22.5% of November 2024's new customers came back within a year, vs 9.0% within 90 days. For occasion buyers the next occasion, not the next 90 days, is the real test.

Recommended Actions

  • Create a Klaviyo segment for November 2025 holiday buyers and split them: gift-for-others vs self-purchasers. The retention strategy is fundamentally different — gift buyers need occasion reminders, self-buyers need product follow-ups.
  • Test switching from percentage discounts to value-adds (free shipping, bonus item, loyalty points) for this buyer profile — discounts attract price-shoppers, value-adds attract brand-engaged buyers.
  • For gift buyers, test a "reminder" flow triggered 2–3 weeks before the next major gifting occasion (birthday, anniversary, holiday) with a curated "gifts they'll love" collection.
