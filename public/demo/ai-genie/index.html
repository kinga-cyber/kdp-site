The Problem

Every agency hits the same wall with Klaviyo + AI.

MCP gave you access. It didn't give you a production system. Here's where things stall:

Stage 1 · Excitement

Claude can read my Klaviyo data!

You connect MCP, ask a few questions, get numbers back. It works. Everyone on the team is impressed. You start imagining what's possible.

Stage 2 · Frustration

Wait — why do the answers keep changing?

Same question, different sessions, different interpretations. The AI doesn't know what "good" means in your client's account. Open rates go up — is that good? Depends on lifecycle stage. The AI doesn't know that.

Stage 3 · The Wall

No scheduling. No security between accounts. No way to run it continuously. No structured output you'd trust in a client report. Your prototype stays a prototype — and your billable hours keep going to manual work.
