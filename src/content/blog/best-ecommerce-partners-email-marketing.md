## 11 Best Apps for Building Up Your Klaviyo Ecosystem

When it comes to email marketing, Klaviyo stands out as a leading marketing automation platform. It empowers eCommerce businesses to create personalized email and SMS campaigns that deliver exceptional results.

The platform offers drag-and-drop email builders, pre-built templates, and customizable sign-up forms. A key strength is its seamless integration with eCommerce platforms including Shopify, WooCommerce, and Magento.

## Featured Partners

### 1. Loyalty Lion: Customer Loyalty Platform

This engagement tool helps businesses create and manage loyalty programs with points-based rewards, referral programs, and VIP tiers to increase retention and drive revenue growth.

### 2. Attentive: Mobile Messaging Platform

Described as "the #1 text message marketing solution driving 19% of total online revenue for over 8,000 leading brands," Attentive offers SMS-first capabilities with audience segmentation and personalization features.

### 3. Okendo: Customer Review Platform

Okendo enables businesses to collect and display customer reviews, gathering authentic feedback and increasing social proof through customizable review requests and moderation.

### 4. Octane AI: Customer Engagement Platform

This quiz platform allows businesses to create interactive quizzes that boost customer engagement and foster brand loyalty through entertaining and educational content.

### 5. Wonderment: Post-Purchase Experience

Wonderment improves post-purchase experiences through proactive shipping notifications and branded tracking pages with Klaviyo integration.

### 6. Recharge: Subscription Management

Recharge handles subscription-based services with flexible plans, automated billing, and subscriber management capabilities.

### 7. MentionMe: Referral Platform

MentionMe incentivizes customer referrals through customizable programs with real-time reporting and A/B testing functionality.

### 8. Rebuy: Personalization Service

Rebuy offers hyper-personalized product recommendations, post-purchase upsells, and checkout customizations to enhance customer lifetime value.

### 9. Lifetimely: Analytics Tool

This platform provides in-depth customer lifetime value analytics to optimize marketing strategies.

### 10. Gorgias: Customer Support

Gorgias centralizes communication channels for efficient customer inquiry management and personalized support experiences.

### 11. Typeform: Survey and Forms Tool

Typeform creates interactive surveys and forms to gather valuable customer feedback.

## Conclusion

Building the right ecosystem around Klaviyo can dramatically improve your email marketing results. Each of these partners brings unique capabilities that complement Klaviyo's core functionality, helping you create more personalized, engaging, and effective campaigns.
