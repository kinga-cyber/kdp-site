## About Geske

Geske is an innovative beauty brand that impressed attendees at Cosmoprof Bologna with its elegant, modern aesthetic. The company's products feature sleek, minimalist packaging combined with beautiful textures and colors. What sets Geske apart is their integration of artificial intelligence and machine learning to personalize products for individual customers, offering a unique and effective experience.

## Geske ft. Technology

Personalization represents a significant trend in email marketing, and Geske elevates this approach through advanced technology. According to research, "91% of consumers are more likely to shop with brands that provide personalized recommendations" and personalization can increase sales by up to 15%.

Geske's AI and machine learning technology analyzes each customer's skin type, preferences, and specific concerns to recommend customized skincare and makeup products, which are created on-site. This delivers a truly individualized beauty experience. Additionally, the brand's commitment to sustainability includes eco-friendly packaging, cruelty-free formulations, and vegan products—values that resonate with today's conscious consumers.

## My Thoughts

My experience with Geske at Cosmoprof demonstrated the beauty industry's future direction. Technology is already personalizing products and experiences, with innovative brands like Geske leading the way. Upon their spring 2023 launch, Geske's combination of cutting-edge technology, sustainability commitment, and affordable pricing (starting at $29.95) positions them for significant market success.

## What are your next steps?

Beauty and cosmetics marketers should incorporate personalization and ethical practices into their strategies, leveraging technology to create distinctive customer experiences while reflecting brand values around sustainability and responsibility.
